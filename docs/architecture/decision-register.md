## Status
Draft. Created 2026-10-16 from `CLAUDE.md`, `architect.md`, `data-model.md`, `invariants.md`, `threat-model.md`, ADR-001, and specs 001–007. `docs/requirements/` was not reviewed; requirement IDs cited by the specs are unverified.

## How to use
- The Owner column names the role that makes the decision (Product owner, Security owner, Architect, DevOps lead), not a person. One person can hold several roles; the approver is recorded in the Status column.
- Every decision starts as `Open`. Status values: `Open`, `Proposed`, `Approved` (with date and approver).
- **Rec.** is a proposal only. "Product owner decides" means no recommendation is made on a business policy.
- 🔒 = security decision that needs explicit approval from the security owner before implementation.
- **Blocks** names the slice(s) that must not be implemented while the decision is `Open`. `CLAUDE.md` forbids implementing a feature before its spec and plan are approved.
- When a decision is approved, record it in an ADR or the relevant spec, then update this register.
